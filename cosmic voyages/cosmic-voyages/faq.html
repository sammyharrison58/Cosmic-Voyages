Frequently Asked Questions

Is space travel safe?

Yes, we have a 99.9% safety record. Our ships are equipped with state-of-the-art life support and emergency escape systems.

Do I need special training?

All passengers undergo a mandatory 2-week training program at our facility in Cape Canaveral to prepare for zero-gravity and emergency procedures.

What is the food like?

Our gourmet meal plans are designed by Michelin-star chefs. We offer a variety of options, including vegan and gluten-free meals.

Can I bring my pet?

Currently, only certified service animals are allowed on board, subject to strict quarantine and health checks.

Is there internet access?

Yes, we provide high-speed interplanetary internet via our laser-link network, though there is a significant latency depending on the distance from Earth.
